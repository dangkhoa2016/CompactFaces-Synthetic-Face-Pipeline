PRIVACY & IMAGE REMOVAL

Concerned about a face in the dataset?

Synthetic faces can sometimes look similar to real people. If an image resembles you, or you have another rights concern, contact the maintainer and identify the image for review.

SUBMIT A REQUEST

For image-removal requests, please include the identity code (e.g. CORE:0106), the view (if known) and the reason for your concern.

How requests are handled

  1. You identify the image and explain your concern by email.
  2. The maintainer reviews the request, the related identity and any supporting information.
  3. If appropriate, the identity or images are removed from controlled copies and hosting updates are coordinated. Mirrors or cached third-party copies may require separate follow-up.

Submitting a request does not cause automatic deletion and does not guarantee removal from third-party mirrors.
